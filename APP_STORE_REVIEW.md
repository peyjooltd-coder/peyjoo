## Question 2 — App purpose and target audience

PeyJoo is a local community marketplace for adults in the Vancouver, Canada area (and related Persian-speaking users). It helps people buy and sell goods, post local classifieds (real estate, jobs, services, vehicles, etc.), message other users about listings, share community/mutual-aid posts (Hamyari), and optionally use an adult (19+) dating section.

The problem it solves: there is no simple, local, bilingual (Persian/English) place to post and find nearby listings and community help. The value is free classifieds, in-app chat, safety tools (report/block/delete account), and local community features.
